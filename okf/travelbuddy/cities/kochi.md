# Walk notes

### Chinese Fishing Nets

Cantilevered nets on the waterfront. One lift of the net is the whole visit. It takes 1 hours in Fort Kochi.

### St. Francis Church

The oldest European church in India. Vasco da Gama was first buried here. It takes 1 hours in Fort Kochi.

### Jew Town and the synagogue lane

Spice godowns and the synagogue at the end of the lane. Walk slowly. It takes 2 hours in Fort Kochi.

### Marine Drive

A paved walk facing the backwaters, best at dusk. It takes 1.5 hours in Ernakulam.

### Hill Palace Museum

A royal museum in a park at Tripunithura. Read the labels and it takes the full time. It takes 2.5 hours in Ernakulam.
